Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

finly is a personal budgeting app operated by Makar in Poland for his own use. It is not offered to the public as a commercial product.

What data is processed

If a bank is connected, Enable Banking (an authorised account information service provider) retrieves account balances and transactions after explicit consent, for example through PKO Bank Polski and the IKO app. finly never asks for or stores iPKO login and password.

The app also stores budget categories, allocation rules, and how you sorted transactions. These records live on the user’s device and, when used, on a small private backend that only talks to Enable Banking.

Why

Data is used solely to show remaining amounts in budget envelopes and to refresh transactions after salary. There is no advertising, no sale of data, and no profiling for third parties.

Sharing

Bank data is accessed through Enable Banking under your consent. We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect the bank in the app and revoke consent in your bank or Enable Banking dashboard. To delete local and backend copies, contact the operator using the same email address given in the Enable Banking application form (data protection / GDPR contact).

Contact

Operator: Makar, Poland.
Data protection contact: the email submitted with the Enable Banking application.
